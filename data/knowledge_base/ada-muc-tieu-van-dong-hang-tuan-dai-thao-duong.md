# Physical Activity Targets for Diabetes Management (ADA)

## Primary Recommendation
150 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity weekly, paired with two strength training sessions.

## Intensity Level
"Your breathing hard enough that you're be able to talk, but not sing during your workout."

## Distribution Options
- 30 minutes, five times weekly
- 25 minutes, six times weekly
- 50 minutes, three times weekly

## Minimum Session Duration
Sessions should last 10 minutes or more at moderate intensity for cardiovascular benefit.

## Spacing Recommendation
No more than two days between workout sessions to maximize blood glucose management benefits.

## Benefits Stated
Lowers risk of heart disease and premature death; helps reach blood pressure and blood glucose targets.

## Medication Considerations
Insulin and sulfonylureas increase hypoglycemia risk. Evening workouts combined with these medications may raise overnight hypoglycemia risk — morning/afternoon exercise preferable. Meals 2-3 hours before activity may require adjusting rapid-acting insulin doses.

## For Beginners
Establish foundational goals before reaching the 150-minute target; progress gradually.

## Ghi chú đối chiếu
Khớp với mục 5.1 của 5481/QĐ-BYT (150 phút/tuần, không quá 2 ngày liên tiếp không vận động). ADA có thêm chi tiết về thời điểm tập trong ngày (sáng/chiều tốt hơn tối) khi dùng insulin/sulfonylurea — chi tiết này KHÔNG có trong 5481/QĐ-BYT, đáng bổ sung.
