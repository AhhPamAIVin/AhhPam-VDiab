# ADA Standards of Care 2026: Nutrition Changes Summary

## Key Changes

**Eating Patterns for Type 2 Prevention**
ADA explicitly endorsed Mediterranean-style and low-carbohydrate eating patterns as "evidence-backed for preventing type 2" diabetes, expanding beyond previous management-only guidance.

**Obesity Treatment Nutrition**
New emphasis on monitoring adequate nutritional intake and physical activity during obesity treatment, relevant given GLP-1 receptor agonist use affecting patient eating amounts.

## Unchanged: Diabetes Plate Method
9-inch plate: half non-starchy vegetables, a quarter lean protein, a quarter quality carbohydrates, water or zero-calorie drink. Compatible with Mediterranean, DASH, low-carb, and vegetarian eating-pattern overlays.

## Release Details
Published December 8, 2025, as Supplement 1 to Volume 49 of *Diabetes Care* journal.

## Ghi chú đối chiếu
Xác nhận lại: ADA nghiêng về khuyến khích low-carb hơn (đặc biệt cho phòng ngừa) so với tỷ lệ Glucid 50-60% cố định của 5481/QĐ-BYT. Khi trả lời câu hỏi cần nêu rõ đây là hai cách tiếp cận khác nhau, không tự ý chọn một bên.
