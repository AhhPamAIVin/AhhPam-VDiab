# Vietnam National Nutrition Portal: Food Nutritional Value Lookup Tool

## Tool Purpose
This page provides access to a searchable database for looking up nutritional values of foods. The tool is part of the Vietnam National Nutritional Portal (Cổng Thông Tin Dinh Dưỡng Quốc Gia) operated by the National Institute of Nutrition.

## Search Interface Components

**Search Fields:**
- Keyword/Từ khóa: Free-text search input
- Group/Nhóm: Dropdown category selector
- Energy (Kcal) filter: Range-based filtering

## Food Categories Available
The dropdown menu lists 15 food groups:
- Beverages (Nước giải khát)
- Cereals and processed products
- Tubers and processed products
- Protein-rich seeds, nuts, and processed versions
- Ripe fruits
- Vegetables and edible roots
- Oils, fats, butter
- Meat and processed meat
- Traditional foods
- Seafood and processed seafood
- Eggs and processed eggs
- Dairy and processed dairy
- Canned goods
- Sweets (sugar, pastries, jams, candy)
- Spices and sauces

## Energy Range Filters
Users can filter results by three caloric bands: "0 - 500," "500 - 1000," or "Hơn 1000" (exceeding 1000 kilocalories).

## Action Buttons
The interface includes "Tìm kiếm" (Search) and "Làm mới" (Refresh/Reset) buttons.
